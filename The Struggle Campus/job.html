Young People Facing Employment Difficulties: Intense Competition and Economic Uncertainty as Major Factors

November 21, 2024 — Shanghai — As the global economy fluctuates and job market competition intensifies, an increasing number of young people are struggling to find employment. Despite having strong academic backgrounds and valuable internship experiences, many still face challenges in securing their ideal jobs. Experts suggest that economic uncertainty, changing industry demands, and societal expectations are the main reasons behind this growing issue.

Tough Job Market, Rising Pressure on Young People According to the latest employment survey, the employment rate for university graduates in 2024 has decreased compared to previous years. Although graduates possess strong academic credentials and internship experiences, many have found it difficult to land their ideal positions due to the rising demand for experience in various industries. Competition in sectors such as technology, finance, and media is especially fierce, and many young people feel overwhelmed during their job search. "Today’s youth are under immense job pressure. Even though many graduates have excellent academic records and internship experience, the combination of slower economic growth and industry transitions makes it harder to secure jobs," said employment expert Li Ming (pseudonym).

Economic Uncertainty and Industry Changes Exacerbate Employment Challenges In recent years, global economic uncertainty has had a profound impact on the job market. As globalization accelerates and technological advancements continue, positions in traditional industries are shrinking while new sectors are rapidly growing. However, many young people have not adequately adapted to these industry changes during their university years, leaving them lacking the necessary skills and experience when entering the job market.

Societal Expectations Add Additional Pressure on Youth In addition to economic and industry changes, societal expectations for young people have been rising. Families, schools, and society at large now expect young people to not only have solid academic achievements but also possess practical experience, leadership skills, and the ability to solve complex problems. These multi-faceted demands create confusion and anxiety among many job seekers.

Government and Society Should Work Together to Provide More Support To address this issue, experts suggest that both the government and society need to provide greater support for young people. Initiatives like career planning guidance, increased industry-academic collaboration, and job training programs can all play a role in easing the employment difficulties facing young people. Additionally, universities should strengthen career development guidance to help students better understand industry demands and plan their professional paths in advance.
