Mental Health

Control your mental animal!

Anxiety Disorders

With overwhelming pressure, information overload, and constant worry, more people are experiencing persistent anxiety and stress.

Tips

Practice regular meditation and deep-breathing exercises to reduce tension and improve emotional regulation.

Depression

Prolonged feelings of sadness, hopelessness, and lack of motivation, often triggered by work pressure, personal struggles, or social isolation.

Tips

Seek Internships, Part-time Jobs, or Volunteer Work & Consider learning soft skills such as communication, teamwork, and problem-solving .

Social Anxiety Disorder

Intense fear and unease in social situations, driven by the fear of being judged or rejected.

Tips

Gradually expose yourself to social situations in small steps, and work with a therapist to build confidence and comfort.

Stress-Related Disorders

Stress from overworking, life imbalances, and prolonged tension leads to emotional instability, sleep disturbances, and physical symptoms.

Tips

Learn time management, prioritize self-care, take regular breaks, and avoid overworking to relieve physical and mental strain.
