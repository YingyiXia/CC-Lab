Concept

"The Struggle Campus" is an interactive game-based website that simulates the campus life of modern college students. The goal is to highlight the struggles students face, such as academic pressure, job search difficulties, economic challenges, and the impact of social media.

Interactive methods

Exploration in a 2D Environment: Players control a point representing themselves and explore the virtual campus. This interaction allows players to engage with the environment and simulate the experience of navigating through different aspects of campus life.

Story Triggers and Choices: Upon entering specific areas, a video is triggered, where a character presents a situation. Players then choose between two options, influencing the direction of the story. This decision-making process mimics real-life dilemmas students face and highlights the uncertainty and pressure of choices in life.
Interaction with Other Dots: Players can interact with other small "dots" on the map, which represent other people or events. These interactions are also driven by choices, showing how social dynamics and external pressures influence a student's experience.
Mini-games as Events: When players enter certain blocks on the map, mini-games will appear. These mini-games simulate specific struggles (like academic pressure, time management, or dealing with social media), adding variety and depth to the game while reinforcing the themes of modern student challenges.

Visual Effects

Videos

Emotional Connection: Through character expressions, tone of voice, and other details, videos can convey emotions, allowing players to better feel the psychological pressures and emotional challenges faced by students.

Context Guidance: Videos can directly present complex situations and emotions, such as portraying a student's psychological state under pressure, or introducing background stories through character dialogues. This helps players quickly immerse themselves in the narrative and empathize with the struggles of students.

p5js Animations

Interactivity and Dynamic Expression: p5.js animations can visually display changes based on player choices, allowing players to feel the direct impact of their decisions on the game world.

Flexible Depiction of Struggles: Animation can dynamically showcase multiple challenges faced by students, such as academic pressure, financial stress, or the influence of social media.
